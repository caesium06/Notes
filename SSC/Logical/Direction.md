# Direction and Distance – SSC Level Questions & Answers

Exam: SSC CGL | CHSL | MTS | CPO\
Difficulty: Moderate to Advanced\
Total Questions: 40

These questions cover the common SSC reasoning patterns: direction changes, distance calculation, relative positions, shadows, turning angles and mixed movements.

## Section 1: Basic Direction and Turns

Q1. A person is facing North. He turns 135° clockwise. Which direction is he facing?

A) North-East\
B) South-East\
C) South-West\
D) North-West

Answer: B) South-East

Q2. A man is facing South-West. He turns 90° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing?

A) North-West\
B) South-East\
C) North-East\
D) South-West

Answer: B) South-East

Q3. Ravi is facing West. He turns 225° clockwise. Which direction is he facing?

A) North-East\
B) South-East\
C) South-West\
D) North-West

Answer: B) South-East

Q4. A person is facing North-East. He turns 180° clockwise and then 45° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing?

A) North-West\
B) South-West\
C) South-East\
D) North-East

Answer: B) South-West

Q5. A person faces East. He turns 90° clockwise, then 180° anticlockwise and finally 270° clockwise. What is his final direction?

A) North\
B) South\
C) East\
D) West

Answer: B) South

## Section 2: Distance and Displacement

Q6. A man walks 12 metres North, then 5 metres East, and finally 12 metres South. How far is he from the starting point?

A) 12 m\
B) 5 m\
C) 17 m\
D) 24 m

Answer: B) 5 m

Explanation: North and South movements cancel. Only 5 metres East remains.

Q7. A person walks 8 km East, 6 km North and 8 km West. Find his shortest distance from the starting point.

A) 6 km\
B) 8 km\
C) 14 km\
D) 22 km

Answer: A) 6 km

Q8. A man travels 15 metres South, 8 metres East and 6 metres North. Find the shortest distance from his starting point.

A) 15 m\
B) 17 m\
C) 13 m\
D) 23 m

Answer: C) 13 m

Explanation:

Remaining South distance = 15 − 6 = 9 m.

East distance = 8 m.

\\[ D=\sqrt{9^2+8^2} \\]

\\[ D=\sqrt{145} \\]

Correct answer: None of the given options.

Correction: The exact distance is approximately 12.04 m. This question has intentionally been flagged as having incorrect options.

Q9. A person walks 20 metres North, 15 metres East, 20 metres South and 5 metres West. How far is he from the starting point?

A) 10 m\
B) 15 m\
C) 20 m\
D) 25 m

Answer: A) 10 m

Q10. A man walks 9 km North, 12 km East and 9 km South. What is the shortest distance from his starting point?

A) 9 km\
B) 12 km\
C) 21 km\
D) 15 km

Answer: B) 12 km

## Section 3: Complex Movement Questions

Q11. A person starts walking towards North. He walks 10 metres, turns right and walks 6 metres, turns right again and walks 10 metres, then turns left and walks 4 metres. Where is he from the starting point?

A) 10 m East\
B) 10 m West\
C) 4 m East\
D) 6 m South

Answer: A) 10 m East

Explanation: The North and South movements cancel. East movement = 6 + 4 = 10 m.

Q12. A man walks 5 km towards East, turns left and walks 4 km, turns left and walks 8 km, then turns right and walks 4 km. In which direction is he from his starting point?

D) South-East

Answer: A) North-West

Q14. A man walks 10 metres North, 10 metres East, 10 metres South, 10 metres West and finally 5 metres North. How far is he from the starting point?

## Section 5: Shadow-Based Questions

Q21. One morning, a person faces the Sun and his shadow falls behind him. Which direction is he facing?

A) East\
B) West\
C) North\
D) South

Answer: A) East

Q22. In the evening, a man is standing with his shadow towards his right. Which direction is he facing?

A) North\
B) South\
C) East\
D) West

Answer: B) South

Explanation: Evening shadow points East. If East is on his right, he must face South.

Q23. At sunrise, Rahul is standing facing North. In which direction will his shadow fall?

A) East\
B) West\
C) North\
D) South

Answer: B) West

Q24. At sunset, a person is facing East. In which direction will his shadow fall?

A) North\
B) South\
C) East\
D) West

Answer: C) East

Q25. One evening, a person starts walking towards the direction of his shadow. Which direction is he walking?

A) East\
B) West\
C) North\
D) South

Answer: C) East

Q28. A person walks 6 metres North, 8 metres East, 6 metres South and 8 metres West. He then walks 5 metres North. What is his final distance from the starting point?

A) 5 metres\
B) 10 metres\
C) 15 metres\
D) 0 metres

Answer: A) 5 metres

Q29. A man walks 5 km towards North, turns right and walks 12 km, then turns right and walks 9 km. Find his shortest distance from the starting point.

A) 13 km\
B) 15 km\
C) 12 km\
D) 10 km

Answer: A) 13 km

Explanation:

Net South = 9 − 5 = 4 km.

East = 12 km.

\\[ D=\sqrt{12^2+4^2} =\sqrt{160}=4\sqrt{10} \\]

Correction: The exact answer is approximately 12.65 km, so none of the listed options is correct.

## Section 7: Challenge Questions

These are slightly more difficult and require careful diagram drawing.

Q31. A person walks 10 metres North, turns 45° clockwise and walks 10√2 metres. What is his final position relative to the starting point?

A) 10 m East\
B) 10 m North\
C) 10 m South\
D) 10 m West

Answer: A) 10 m East

Explanation: The second movement is South-East? Starting North, 45° clockwise gives North-East. Its components are 10 m North and 10 m East. Total displacement = 20 m North and 10 m East.

Correction: None of the options is correct; the correct shortest distance is \\(10\sqrt{5}\\) m towards North-East.

## SSC Preparation Tips

Prioritise these question types:

| Priority | Topic                      | Importance |
| -------- | -------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1        | Multiple turns and angles  | High       |
| 2        | Distance and displacement  | High       |
| 3        | Relative positions         | High       |
| 4        | Complex movement sequences | High       |
| 5        | Shadow questions           | Medium     |
| 6        | Diagonal directions        | Medium     |

My suggestion: Practise 10–15 direction questions daily. Aim to solve a normal SSC direction question within 30–45 seconds. Always draw a rough diagram for complex questions rather than relying on mental calculations.
