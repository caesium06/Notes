# Seating Arrangement – SSC CGL / CHSL / CPO

## 1. Important Rules & Shortcuts

| Arrangement               | Key points                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Linear (North-facing)     | Left = West, Right = East                                                |
| Linear (South-facing)     | Left = East, Right = West                                                |
| Circular (Facing Centre)  | Left = Clockwise, Right = Anticlockwise                                  |
| Circular (Facing Outside) | Left = Anticlockwise, Right = Clockwise                                  |
| Square/Rectangle          | Apply centre-facing or outside-facing rules                              |
| Two parallel rows         | Opposite persons face each other; left/right depends on facing direction |

## 2. Important Question Types

- Immediate neighbours: Persons sitting directly beside each other.
- Second to the left/right: One person sits between them.
- Between: Check positions on the same side or direction.
- Extreme ends: First and last positions in a linear arrangement.
- Opposite: Directly facing each other.
- Not adjacent: At least one seat/person between them.

## 3. High-Yield Solving Tricks

1. Fix the person with a definite position first.
2. Use negative clues (not, neither, nor) after placing positive clues.
3. For circular arrangements, fix one person at the top to remove rotational confusion.
4. In parallel rows, draw both rows separately and mark facing directions.
5. Avoid assuming positions unless a clue confirms them.
6. If multiple possibilities exist, draw separate cases.
7. For 8–10 people, use numbered slots to avoid repeatedly redrawing.

## 4. SSC PYQ-Based Practice Questions

Questions below are representative of SSC previous-year question patterns, not claimed as verbatim PYQs.

Q1. Linear Arrangement

Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north.

- A sits second to the left of D.
- B sits immediately right of A.
- C sits at the extreme right.
- E sits immediately left of D.
- F is not adjacent to C.

Who sits at the extreme left?

A) A\
B) B\
C) F\
D) E

Q2. Circular Arrangement

Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit around a circular table facing the centre.

- P sits opposite S.
- Q sits immediately clockwise of P.
- R sits immediately clockwise of Q.
- T sits opposite Q.

Who sits opposite R?

A) P\
B) S\
C) U\
D) T

Q3. Parallel Rows

Five persons A, B, C, D and E sit in Row 1 facing south. Five persons P, Q, R, S and T sit in Row 2 facing north.

- A sits opposite R.
- B sits opposite P.
- C sits immediately right of A.
- D sits opposite T.

Who sits opposite C?

A) P\
B) Q\
C) S\
D) T

Q4. Linear Arrangement

Seven persons sit in a row facing north.

- M is fourth from the left.
- N sits second to the right of M.
- P sits immediately left of M.
- Q sits at the extreme right.

What is N's position from the right?

A) First\
B) Second\
C) Third\
D) Fourth

Q5. Circular Arrangement

Eight persons sit around a circular table facing the centre.

- A sits opposite E.
- B sits immediately clockwise of A.
- C sits second clockwise of A.
- D sits opposite B.

Who sits immediately anticlockwise of E?

A) B\
B) C\
C) D\
D) F

## 5. Answer Key

| Q | Answer |

| 1 | C      |
| 2 | B      |
| 3 | B      |
| 4 | B      |
| 5 | D      |

Exam priority: Linear arrangement, circular arrangement, and two parallel rows. Practise mixed-direction questions carefully, as left/right confusion is a common source of errors.
