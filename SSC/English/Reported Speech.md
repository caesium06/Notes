# Reported Speech: Revision Note



## 1. Definitions

- **Direct speech** quotes the speaker's exact words, inside quotation marks.
  *She said, "I am tired."*
- **Reported (indirect) speech** reports what was said without quoting, often changing tense, pronouns and time words.
  *She said that she was tired.*
- **Reporting verb**: the verb that introduces the speech (*said, told, asked, ordered*).
- **Reported clause**: what was actually said (*that she was tired*).
- **Backshift**: moving the verb one tense "back" when the reporting verb is in the past.

---

## 2. The Four Steps for Converting Direct to Reported Speech

1. Remove the quotation marks and add a connector (*that, if/whether, to*, or a wh-word).
2. Change the **tense** if the reporting verb is past.
3. Change the **pronouns** (and possessives) to match the new viewpoint.
4. Change **time and place expressions** if the context has shifted.

---

## 3. Tense Changes (Backshift)

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|
| Simple present: "I **work** here." | Simple past: He said he **worked** there. |
| Present continuous: "I **am working**." | Past continuous: He said he **was working**. |
| Present perfect: "I **have finished**." | Past perfect: He said he **had finished**. |
| Present perfect continuous: "I **have been waiting**." | Past perfect continuous: He said he **had been waiting**. |
| Simple past: "I **saw** it." | Past perfect: He said he **had seen** it. |
| Past continuous: "I **was sleeping**." | Past perfect continuous: He said he **had been sleeping**. |
| Past perfect: "I **had left**." | Past perfect (no change): He said he **had left**. |
| Future *will*: "I **will go**." | *would*: He said he **would go**. |
| Future *is going to*: "I **am going to leave**." | *was going to*: He said he **was going to leave**. |

### Modal verbs

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| can | could |
| may | might |
| will | would |
| shall | would / should |
| must | had to (obligation); must (logical deduction) |
| could, would, should, might, ought to | no change |

---

## 4. Pronoun Changes

Pronouns change according to who is speaking and who is being spoken to.

- *Ravi said, "**I** like **my** job."* → Ravi said that **he** liked **his** job.
- *She said to him, "**I** will help **you**."* → She told him that **she** would help **him**.
- *"**We** have done **our** homework," they said.* → They said that **they** had done **their** homework.

---

## 5. Time and Place Words

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| now | then / at that time |
| today | that day |
| tonight | that night |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| last week / month / year | the week / month / year before; the previous week... |
| next week / month / year | the following week / month / year |
| ago | before |
| here | there |
| this / these | that / those |
| come | go (depending on location) |

## 6. Types of Sentences in Reported Speech

### 6.1 Statements
- Use *said (that)* or *told + person (that)*.
- Direct: *"I am leaving tomorrow," she said.*
- Reported: *She said (that) she was leaving the next day.*
- **Rule:** *say* is not followed directly by a person (*said to him*), but *tell* always is (*told him*).

### 6.2 Yes/No Questions
- Connector: **if** or **whether**.
- Use **statement word order** (subject before verb); no *do/does/did*; no question mark.
- Direct: *"Do you like tea?" he asked.*
- Reported: *He asked if I liked tea.*

### 6.3 Wh-Questions
- Keep the wh-word (*what, where, when, why, how, who*).
- Use statement word order and no question mark.
- Direct: *"Where do you live?" she asked.*
- Reported: *She asked where I lived.*

### 6.4 Commands and Requests
- Use **to + infinitive** (positive) or **not to + infinitive** (negative).
- Reporting verbs: *told, ordered, commanded, asked, requested, begged, advised, warned*.
- Direct: *"Close the door," he said.* → He **told** me **to close** the door.
- Direct: *"Please help me," she said.* → She **asked** me **to help** her.
- Direct: *"Don't touch it," he said.* → He **warned** me **not to touch** it.

### 6.5 Suggestions
- *"Let's go to the park," he said.* → He **suggested going** to the park. / He suggested that we **should go** to the park.

### 6.6 Exclamations and Wishes
- *"What a beautiful day!" she said.* → She **exclaimed that** it was a beautiful day. / She **exclaimed with joy that**...
- *"Hurrah! We won!"* → They **shouted with joy that** they had won.
- *"May God bless you!"* → He **prayed that** God might bless me.

### 6.7 Greetings, Thanks, Apologies
- *"Good morning," he said.* → He **wished** me good morning.
- *"Thank you," she said.* → She **thanked** me.
- *"Sorry," he said.* → He **apologised**.

---

## 7. When NOT to Change the Tense

Backshift is optional or unnecessary in these cases:

1. **The reporting verb is present, present perfect or future.**
   *He says, "I am busy."* → He says that he **is** busy.
2. **The statement is a universal or scientific truth.**
   *"The Earth revolves around the Sun," the teacher said.* → The teacher said that the Earth **revolves** around the Sun.
3. **The situation is still true or unchanged.**
   *"I live in Kochi," he said.* → He said he **lives** (or **lived**) in Kochi.
4. **The verb is already past perfect**, or is a modal that doesn't change (*would, could, should, might, ought to*).
5. **Unreal conditionals** (second and third conditional) usually remain unchanged.
   *"If I were rich, I would travel," he said.* → He said that if he **were** rich, he **would** travel.

---

## 8. Reporting Verbs: Quick Reference

| Pattern | Verbs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| verb + **that** clause | say, claim, explain, admit, deny, announce, complain, promise | He **admitted that** he had made a mistake. |
| verb + **person + to-infinitive** | tell, ask, order, advise, warn, remind, invite, beg | She **advised him to rest**. |
| verb + **to-infinitive** | promise, offer, refuse, agree, threaten | He **promised to call**. |
| verb + **-ing** | suggest, deny, admit, recommend | She **denied taking** the money. |
| verb + **preposition + -ing** | apologise for, insist on, accuse of, congratulate on, thank for | He **accused her of lying**. |

---

## 9. Special Cases

- **Mixed speech** (a statement plus a question or command): split it into two clauses and use appropriate connectors and reporting verbs.
  *He said to me, "I am tired. Will you help me?"* → He told me that he was tired and asked if I would help him.
- **Multiple questions:** report each one with its own connector.
- **Imperative with "let":** *"Let him go," she said.* → She **ordered that he be allowed to go.**
- **"Must" for deduction does not change:** *"He must be at home."* → She said he **must be** at home.
- **"Had better":** no change in form.
- **Reporting after a long time:** you may keep the present tense if the information is still true.

---

## 10. Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Writing *He said me* → correct: *He **told** me* or *He **said to** me*.
2. Keeping question word order: *She asked where **did I live*** → *She asked where **I lived***.
3. Forgetting to change pronouns or time words.
4. Using *that* with questions: *She asked that where I lived* is wrong.
5. Keeping a question mark in reported questions.
6. Changing tense when the statement is a universal truth.
7. Using *to* after *suggest*: *suggested to go* is wrong → *suggested going*.

## 11. Worked Examples (Practice Set)

1. *"I am reading a novel," she said.* → She said that she **was reading** a novel.
2. *"We have finished the project," they said.* → They said that they **had finished** the project.
3. *He said, "I will call you tomorrow."* → He said that he **would call** me **the next day**.
4. *"Where are you going?" he asked her.* → He asked her **where she was going**.
5. *"Do you know him?" she asked.* → She asked **if/whether I knew him**.
6. *"Don't make noise," the teacher said to the students.* → The teacher **told the students not to make noise**.
7. *"Let's play cricket," said Arun.* → Arun **suggested playing** cricket.
8. *"Hurrah! We have won the match!" they said.* → They **exclaimed with joy that they had won** the match.
9. *"The sun rises in the east," said the teacher.* → The teacher said that the sun **rises** in the east.
10. *"Can you swim?" he asked me.* → He asked me **if I could swim**.

## 12. Last-Minute Revision Checklist

- [ ] Is the reporting verb past? If yes, backshift the tense.
- [ ] Have pronouns been adjusted?
- [ ] Have time and place words been changed?
- [ ] Questions: **if/whether** or the wh-word, with statement word order, no "?".
- [ ] Commands: **to / not to + infinitive**, with a suitable reporting verb.
- [ ] Universal truths and still-true facts: **no change**.
- [ ] Used *tell + person* and *say (to + person)* correctly.
