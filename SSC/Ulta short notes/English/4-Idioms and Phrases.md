## 1. Core Concept / Definition

**Percentage** means a fraction or ratio expressed out of 100.

**Basic Formula:**

- Percentage = (Value ÷ Total) × 100
- Value = (Percentage × Total) ÷ 100
- Total = (Value × 100) ÷ Percentage

## 3. Important Percentage Formulas

### A. Percentage Increase and Decrease

| Situation | Formula |
|---|---|
| Increase by x% | Original × (100 + x) ÷ 100 |
| Decrease by x% | Original × (100 - x) ÷ 100 |
| Percentage increase | (Increase ÷ Original) × 100 |
| Percentage decrease | (Decrease ÷ Original) × 100 |

**Example:** A number increases from 200 to 250.

Percentage increase = ((250 - 200) ÷ 200) × 100

= (50 ÷ 200) × 100

= **25%**

### B. Successive Percentage Change

When a value changes by two successive percentages:

**Net change (%) = a + b + (a × b)/100**

- Increase = Positive (+)
- Decrease = Negative (-)

**Example:** Increase by 20%, then decrease by 10%.

= 20 - 10 + (20 × -10)/100

= 10 - 2

= **8% increase**

### C. Equal Percentage Increase and Decrease

If a number increases by x% and then decreases by x%:

**Net loss (%) = x² ÷ 100**

**Example:** Increase by 20%, then decrease by 20%.

= 20² ÷ 100

= 400 ÷ 100

= **4% decrease**

### D. Comparison of Two Numbers

If A is x% more than B:

A = B × (100 + x) ÷ 100

Percentage by which B is less than A:

= [x ÷ (100 + x)] × 100

If A is x% less than B:

Percentage by which B is more than A:

= [x ÷ (100 - x)] × 100

**Example:** A is 25% more than B.

B is less than A by:

= (25 ÷ 125) × 100

= **20%**

### E. Population Increase / Depreciation

**Final Population = P × (1 ± r/100)ⁿ**

Where:

- P = Initial population/value
- r = Annual growth or depreciation rate
- n = Number of years
- (+) = Growth
- (-) = Depreciation

### F. Income, Expenditure and Savings

**Savings = Income - Expenditure**

- Calculate new income and expenditure separately.
- Find new savings by subtracting expenditure from income.
- Percentage change in savings is calculated on original savings, NOT original income.

## 4. Shortcuts / Memory Tricks

### A. Fast Percentage Calculations

| Percentage | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| 10% of N | N ÷ 10 |
| 5% of N | 10% ÷ 2 |
| 1% of N | N ÷ 100 |
| 20% of N | N ÷ 5 |
| 25% of N | N ÷ 4 |
| 50% of N | N ÷ 2 |
| 75% of N | 50% + 25% |
| 12.5% of N | N ÷ 8 |
| 33.33% of N | N ÷ 3 |
| 16.67% of N | N ÷ 6 |

### B. x% of y = y% of x

**Formula:**

x% of y = y% of x

**Example:**

16% of 25 = 25% of 16

= 4

### C. Fraction-Based Calculation

Convert percentages into fractions before multiplying.

**Example:** Find 37.5% of 640.

37.5% = 3/8

= (3 ÷ 8) × 640

= **240**

### D. Percentage Change in Price and Consumption

If price increases by x%, the reduction in consumption required to maintain the same expenditure is:

**Reduction (%) = [x ÷ (100 + x)] × 100**

If price decreases by x%, the increase in consumption possible at the same expenditure is:

**Increase (%) = [x ÷ (100 - x)] × 100**

**Example:** Price increases by 25%.

Required consumption reduction:

= (25 ÷ 125) × 100

= **20%**

### E. Reversing Percentage Changes

If A is 20% more than B, then B is 16.67% less than A.

**Remember:** The base changes when the comparison is reversed.

## 5. Top 3 PYQ Traps

1. **Base value matters:** 20% increase followed by 20% decrease does NOT give zero change. It results in a 4% loss.
2. **Comparison reversal:** If A is 25% more than B, B is 20% less than A, NOT 25%.
3. **Percentage points vs percentage change:** An increase from 20% to 25% is a 5 percentage-point increase but a 25% relative increase.

## 6. High-Yield Practice Questions

**Q1.** A number is increased by 25% and subsequently decreased by 20%. Find the net percentage change.

**Q2.** The price of sugar increases by 25%. By what percentage must a family reduce its consumption to maintain the same expenditure?

**Q3.** A's income is 40% more than B's income. By what percentage is B's income less than A's income?

**Q4.** The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. If the initial population was 50,000, find the population after two years and the net percentage change.

## 7. Answer Key & Explanations

**Q1. Answer: 0% (No change)**

Net change = 25 - 20 + (25 × -20)/100

= 5 - 5

= **0%**

The successive increase and decrease cancel each other exactly.

**Q2. Answer: 20%**

Required reduction = [25 ÷ (100 + 25)] × 100

= (25 ÷ 125) × 100

= **20%**

Consumption must decrease by 20% to maintain the same expenditure.

**Q3. Answer: 28.57%**

Required percentage = [40 ÷ (100 + 40)] × 100

= (40 ÷ 140) × 100

= **28.57%**

**Q4. Answer: 49,500; 1% decrease**

Final population:

= 50,000 × (110 ÷ 100) × (90 ÷ 100)

= 50,000 × 1.1 × 0.9

= **49,500**

Net change:

= 10 - 10 + (10 × -10)/100

= -1%

Therefore, the population decreases by **1%**.
